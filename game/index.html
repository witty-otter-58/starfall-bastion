Starfall Bastion

The void is coming for the last starlight. Build towers on the field, stop twenty waves, and keep the core alive.

Build: pick a tower, click an empty tile. Click a tower to upgrade or sell it.
Defend: creatures follow the path to the core on the right. Anything that reaches it costs lives.
Win: clear wave 20. Lose: the core falls. Gold comes from kills and cleared waves.
Keys: 1–5 towers · Space next wave · F speed · P pause · M mute · U upgrade · X sell
